| One alternate pack shot per product | 1600 × 2000 px | 4:5 | Product galleries |
| Six Wynn Method stage images | 1400 × 1400 px | 1:1 | Method section |
| Six style campaign images | 1400 × 1000 px | 7:5 | Shop by Style |
| Ten ingredient photographs | 1200 × 1200 px | 1:1 | Ingredient library |
| `healthy-hair-campaign.jpg` | 2000 × 1400 px | 10:7 | Hair campaign |

Images should feature Wynn Essentials products or commissioned brand photography. Do not substitute competitor photography. Provide descriptive alt text and usage rights with final files.
